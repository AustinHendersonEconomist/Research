Using this in a paper

The chapters fit a 12-week 300- or 400-level health economics paper: one chapter per week, plus weeks for the CEA project and revision. Each chapter supports a 20-minute in-class activity. Ask students to predict before they move a slider, then explain any surprise. The discussion prompts work as tutorial questions or short essays.

All numbers are illustrative. Models are simplified for teaching and noted on each page.
